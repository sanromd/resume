Career Arc

Phase I — Theoretical & computational photonics (KAUST, 2009–2014). Founding cohort member of the KAUST Photonics Master's programme; M.S. (2010) and PhD (2014) in Electrical Engineering under Prof. Boon S. Ooi. Work centred on light propagation in heterogeneous, nonlinear and time-varying media — computational electromagnetics, beam shaping and chalcogenide-glass material engineering. Thesis: Exploring Heterogeneous and Time-Varying Materials for Photonic Applications, Towards Solutions for the Manipulation and Confinement of Light (KAUST, 2014).

Phase II — Applied laser–matter interaction (Saudi Aramco, 2015–2019). Transition from theory to industrial application: numerical modelling of laser–rock and electromagnetic–rock interaction, thermal/geomechanical coupling, and the early laser-perforation programme. First-author work established the physics models underpinning the technology.

Phase III — Technology deployment & diversification (2020–present). Co-PI on Aramco's high-power laser programme with Sameeh I. Batarseh, carrying the technology from laboratory demonstration to first industrial field deployment. In parallel, opened three new research lines: hydrogen-sulfide valorisation and solar fuels; photonic and machine-learning sensing for subsurface and surface assets; and hollow-core optical fibre for kilowatt-class downhole power delivery.

Achievement Clusters

High-power laser technology: laboratory to first industrial deployment

The defining programme of the career, documented continuously in the peer-reviewed and industry record over a decade:

  • Physics & modelling foundation (2016–2019) — first-authored the governing numerical treatments of laser–rock interaction (ARMA-2016-142), thermal/geomechanical dynamics of electromagnetic heating (SPE-183917-MS, SPE-188790-MS), and a scalable numerical solution for subsurface wave propagation (SPE-194888-MS, sole author). The coupled thermal–mechanical model was published in SPE Journal (2019).
  • Technology maturation (2019–2022) — laser perforation as a completion technology (SPE-197192-MS), performance in carbonates and organic-rich shales (SPE-197480-MS; SPE-204809-MS, first author), laser drilling in extremely hard formations (OTC-30416-MS), and laser cladding for corrosion prevention (SPE-201617-MS).
  • First field deployments (2021–2024) — first industrial flowline-descaling deployment (Dec. 2021; SPE-209972-MS), underlying principles first-authored (SPE-209977-MS); first industrial high-power laser perforation deployment (SPE-215016-MS, 2023) and its field validation (SPE-220900-MS, 2024).
  • Extension & scale-up (2023–2025) — laser drilling milestones (SPE-215166-MS), photonic–hydraulic hybrid fracturing lowering breaking pressure (SPE-216510-MS, SPE-215162-MS), a long-range descaling robot (SPE-222608-MS), sweet-spot creation in tight/unconventional reservoirs (SPE-227837-MS, 2025), and net-zero reframing of the programme (SPE-224865-MS, 2025).

Hollow-core optical fibre for kilowatt-class power delivery

Co-inventor with the University of Southampton Optoelectronics Research Centre (Poletti/Richardson group) on nested anti-resonant nodeless fibre (NANF) and corrugated-cladding fibre enabling multi-kilowatt continuous-wave transmission at 1050–1100 nm over the distances required for downhole delivery — the enabling physical layer for the laser programme above (US 11,555,958 B2 + EP 4,067,952 B1; US 11,585,976 B2 + EP 4,067,735 A1 + SA 122,430,881 B1).

Hydrogen sulfide valorisation and solar fuels

A sustainability research line combining peer-reviewed publication with a protected IP position — converting a waste stream into hydrogen rather than elemental sulfur: senior-author perspective in Advanced Energy and Sustainability Research (2023, with the KAUST Laquai group); UV-induced decomposition pathways in RSC Advances (2025, up to 52% conversion within 60 minutes); and a patent cluster spanning photocatalytic conversion (US 12,559,366 B2, lead inventor), dissociation via reactive sulfur species (US 12,459,816 B2), photolytic conversion in produced-gas streams (US 2024/0060411 A1), and steam-methane reforming (US 2025/0368507 A1).

Photonic and machine-learning sensing

A coherent programme applying optics, spectroscopy and machine learning to characterisation and monitoring: subsurface property inference from early ML applications (SPE-195118-MS, 2019, first author) to hyperspectral multiphysics inference (SPIE, 2023, first author), protected in US 12,682,216 B2 (sole inventor) and US 12,487,075 B2; asset monitoring and computer vision (IPTC-23632-MS; US 12,430,795 B2; US 11,725,504 B2, five-jurisdiction family; the 2026 wellhead-displacement trio using beam deflection, speckle reflectometry and shadowgraphy); emissions/sustainability (US 12,394,183 B2); and chemical/fibre sensing (US 11,867,629 B2; US 12,253,459 B2; US 12,359,554 B2; US 2024/0141777 A1).

Theoretical physics and computational electromagnetics

The scientific foundation of the career: optical analogues of gravitational attractors (Optics Express, 2013, first author — the most-cited work, featured in Laser Focus World, July 2013); Bessel–Gauss beam shaping from heterogeneous refractive-index maps (JOSA A, 2012, first author); electron-irradiation-induced permittivity reduction in chalcogenide glass (J. Appl. Phys., 2013, first author); a high-order finite-volume method for Maxwell's equations in time-varying media (arXiv:2307.11842, first author, with D. I. Ketcheson, 2023); and a revisitation of the quantum beam splitter without a vacuum state (Rev. Mex. Fís. E, 2022).
