Professional Experience

Applied Physicist — Co-PI, High-Power Laser Programme

2015 – Present · Upstream Advanced Research Center, Saudi Aramco, Dhahran, KSA
Co-led the physics, modelling and field-execution programme that took high-power laser perforation and descaling from laboratory concept to the petroleum industry's first industrial deployments (2021–2024), documented across 25+ peer-reviewed and conference publications and a supporting patent portfolio.
  • Physics & modelling foundation (2016–2019): first-authored the governing numerical treatments of laser–rock interaction and the thermal/geomechanical dynamics of electromagnetic heating; the coupled thermal–mechanical model was published in SPE Journal (2019).
  • First-of-kind field deployments (2021–2024): delivered the industry's first industrial flowline-descaling field trial (Dec. 2021) and first industrial high-power laser perforation deployment (2023), each with a companion field-validation study.
  • Diversification (2020–present): opened three new research lines — hydrogen-sulfide valorisation to hydrogen, photonic/ML sensing for subsurface and surface assets, and hollow-core optical fibre for kilowatt-class downhole power delivery, developed with the University of Southampton Optoelectronics Research Centre.
  • Recognised by Saudi Aramco Senior Vice President Waleed A. Al-Mulhim, with the Laser Research Team, for the first laser-perforation field trial (The Arabian Sun / Aramco Life, 2023).

Graduate Researcher, Photonics Laboratory

2009 – 2014 · KAUST, Thuwal, KSA · Advisor: Prof. Boon S. Ooi
  • PhD research on light propagation in heterogeneous, nonlinear and time-varying media — computational electromagnetics, beam shaping and chalcogenide-glass material engineering.
  • Founding member of the KAUST Photonics Master's programme; developed a high-performance numerical toolbox for wave propagation in complex media (Maxwell-GEMS).
